בית המקדשBeit HaMikdach

The Temple of Jerusalem, rebuilt in three dimensions to the measurements of the Mishnah. You enter on foot, walk where you like, and every stone tells you where it comes from.

Enter the tourFree, nothing to install. Ten minutes or an hour.

Shulchan Arukh, Orach Chayim 95:2

צריך שיכוף ראשו מעט שיהיו עיניו למטה לארץ ויחשוב כאלו עומד בבית המקדש ובלבו יכוין למעלה לשמים

He should bow his head slightly, his eyes cast down toward the earth, and consider himself as if standing in the Beit HaMikdash; and in his heart direct himself upward, toward heaven.

The lishkot

Around the courts, seventeen chambers, each with its own service. The Sanhedrin sits in one, hides are salted in another, the Kohen Gadol immerses on a rooftop. Every one of them is built in the tour.

The degrees of holiness

From the Har HaBayit to the Kodesh HaKodashim, Mishnah Kelim counts the degrees of holiness. At each one, fewer may enter.

  1. 1Har HaBayit
  2. 2Chel
  3. 3Ezrat Nashim
  4. 4Ezrat Israel
  5. 5Ezrat Kohanim
  6. 6Between the Ulam and the altar
  7. 7Heikhal
  8. 8Kodesh HaKodashim

  1. Har HaBayit הר הבית

    Holier than Jerusalem: zavim, zavot, menstruants and women after childbirth may not enter.

    Mishnah Kelim 1:8

    See it in the tour

  2. Chel חיל

    Holier than the Har HaBayit: non-Jews and those made impure by a corpse may not enter.

    Mishnah Kelim 1:8

    See it in the tour

  3. Ezrat Nashim עזרת נשים

    Holier than the Chel: one who immersed that same day may not enter before sunset.

    Mishnah Kelim 1:8

    See it in the tour

  4. Ezrat Israel עזרת ישראל

    Holier than the Ezrat Nashim: one who still owes an offering of atonement may not enter.

    Mishnah Kelim 1:8

    See it in the tour

  5. Ezrat Kohanim עזרת כהנים

    Holier than the Ezrat Israel: Israelites enter only for what requires them, the semikhah, the shechitah and the tenufah.

    Mishnah Kelim 1:8

    See it in the tour

  6. Between the Ulam and the altar בין האולם ולמזבח

    Holier than the Ezrat Kohanim: priests with a blemish or unkempt hair may not enter.

    Mishnah Kelim 1:9

    See it in the tour

  7. Heikhal היכל

    Holier still: no one enters without having washed hands and feet.

    Mishnah Kelim 1:9

    See it in the tour

Berakhot 30a

היה עומד בחוץ לארץ יכוין את לבו כנגד ארץ ישראל… היה עומד בבית קדשי הקדשים יכוין את לבו כנגד בית הכפורת…
נמצאו כל ישראל מכוונין את לבם למקום אחד

One standing outside the Land of Israel directs his heart toward the Land of Israel; in the Land of Israel, toward Jerusalem; in Jerusalem, toward the Beit HaMikdash; in the Beit HaMikdash, toward the Kodesh HaKodashim; in the Kodesh HaKodashim, toward the Beit HaKaporet… Thus all Israel direct their hearts toward one place.

Kodesh HaKodashim קודש הקודשים

Holiest of all: no one enters but the Kohen Gadol, on Yom Kippur, at the hour of the service.

Mishnah Kelim 1:9
