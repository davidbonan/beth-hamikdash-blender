בית המקדש

Beit HaMikdach

The Temple of Jerusalem rebuilt in 3D, to scale, from the Mishnah Middot. Walk freely from the Har HaBayit to the Kodesh HaKodashim.

Enter the tourrecent browser, keyboard or touch

Walk through the Temple

Courtyards, gates, chambers, the altar, the Heikhal: everything is built in volumes at the measurements of the sources, and you move through it as a visitor would.

Every element has its source

Click a gate, a vessel or a garment to learn what it is, its measure, and the text behind it: Middot, Tamid, Yoma, Rambam, with a link to Sefaria.

The choices are stated

Where the sources disagree or fall silent, the tour says which opinion was kept, which was set aside, and what is a choice of the project.

Which Temple?

The one to come, which is neither the First nor the Second. The plan and the measurements come from Middot, the only measured description of a standing Temple. What the First Temple had without Middot repeating it returns when the Tanakh describes it and nothing abolishes it: the Ark is back on the Even HaShetiya, Yakhin and Boaz stand in the Ulam.

The scene is generated by script in Blender, and the tour is its export. The figures, kohanim and Levi'im, are dressed after the Rambam, Klei HaMikdash.
