The degrees of holiness

From the Har HaBayit to the Kodesh HaKodashim, Mishnah Kelim counts the degrees of holiness. At each one, fewer may enter.

  1. 1Har HaBayit
  2. 2Chel
  3. 3Ezrat Nashim
  4. 4Ezrat Israel
  5. 5Ezrat Kohanim
  6. 6Between the Ulam and the altar
  7. 7Heikhal
  8. 8Kodesh HaKodashim
  1. See it in the tour

    Har HaBayit הר הבית

    Holier than Jerusalem: zavim, zavot, menstruants and women after childbirth may not enter.

    Mishnah Kelim 1:8

  2. See it in the tour

    Chel חיל

    Holier than the Har HaBayit: non-Jews and those made impure by a corpse may not enter.

    Mishnah Kelim 1:8

  3. See it in the tour

    Ezrat Nashim עזרת נשים

    Holier than the Chel: one who immersed that same day may not enter before sunset.

    Mishnah Kelim 1:8

  4. See it in the tour

    Ezrat Israel עזרת ישראל

    Holier than the Ezrat Nashim: one who still owes an offering of atonement may not enter.

    Mishnah Kelim 1:8

  5. See it in the tour

    Ezrat Kohanim עזרת כהנים

    Holier than the Ezrat Israel: Israelites enter only for what requires them, the semikhah, the shechitah and the tenufah.

    Mishnah Kelim 1:8

  6. See it in the tour

    Between the Ulam and the altar בין האולם ולמזבח

    Holier than the Ezrat Kohanim: priests with a blemish or unkempt hair may not enter.

    Mishnah Kelim 1:9

  7. See it in the tour

    Heikhal היכל

    Holier still: no one enters without having washed hands and feet.

    Mishnah Kelim 1:9

Kodesh HaKodashim קודש הקודשים

Holiest of all: no one enters but the Kohen Gadol, on Yom Kippur, at the hour of the service.

Mishnah Kelim 1:9
